Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine

Selected prizes with the landmark paper behind each, its DOI, the laureates on Wikipedia, and how the discovery was made.

Each paper is a key publication behind the prize, not necessarily the only one cited by the Nobel Committee. Only the 1978 DOI and the 1953 and 1994 citations have been checked against a source so far; the other DOIs were entered from memory, so treat them with care. Early papers (before the DOI era) have none. Every year with a prize from 1901 to 2025 is covered (none was awarded in 1915–18, 1921, 1925 and 1940–42). Entries without a paper title are summarised by the laureate's body of work. Entries marked "Not yet verified" have no DOI checked.
